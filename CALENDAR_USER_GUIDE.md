The calendar uses **color intensity** to show your activity level:

| Color | Activity Count | Meaning |
|-------|---------------|---------|
| ⚪ Gray | 0 activities | No activity |
| 🟢 Light Green | 1-2 activities | Light activity |
| 🟢 Green | 3-4 activities | Moderate activity |
| 🟢 Dark Green | 5+ activities | High activity |

---

## 📊 What Counts as Activity?

Your calendar tracks **three types of activities**:

### 1. 🔐 Login Activity
- When you log into the platform
- Counts once per day

### 2. 💻 Practice Activity
- When you open a problem
- When you run code (without completing)
- When you attempt a problem

### 3. ✅ Solve Activity
- When you successfully solve a problem
- When all test cases pass
- Contributes to your streak

**Note**: Multiple activities on the same day are counted together!

### Metrics Explained:

- **Current Streak**: Consecutive days with activity
- **Days Logged In**: Total days you've logged in
- **Total Solved**: Total problems solved (all time)
- **This Month**: Activity count for current month only
- **Active Days**: Days with at least one activity

## ❓ FAQ

### Q: Why are some dates lighter?
**A**: Those are padding days from the previous/next month to complete the calendar grid.

### Q: Can I see previous months?
**A**: Currently shows only the current month. Historical data is preserved in your profile.

### Q: What if I miss a day?
**A**: Your streak resets, but your total activity count remains. Start a new streak!

### Q: Do contest activities count?
**A**: Yes! Contest participation counts as activity.

### Q: Can I see hourly activity?
**A**: The calendar shows daily totals. Individual activities are logged in your profile.
